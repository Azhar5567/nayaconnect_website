    Hiring Models & Delivery

    Flexible recruitment models for FinTech hiring.

    We partner with FinTech startups, scaleups, and digital finance companies to deliver targeted recruitment across engineering, cloud, data, product, and technology leadership.

    Engagement Options

    Retained & Contingent Recruitment Models.

    We structure our engagement based on your role urgency, seniority, and technical specialization requirements.

    Retained Search

    Dedicated search for CTOs, VPs of Engineering, and senior leadership requiring priority execution.

    • Dedicated Senior Partner delivery
    • In-depth talent mapping of target FinTechs
    • Confidential, direct outreach
    • Technical competency & culture assessment
    • Guaranteed search completion

    Contingent Recruitment

    Fast-paced recruitment for software developers, product managers, and technical specialists.

    • Immediate access to pre-screened talent
    • Fast shortlist turnaround
    • Direct assessment against relevant tech stacks
    • Success-aligned fee structure
    • Flexible deployment across all levels
    Our Process

    The 5-Stage Recruitment Methodology.

    STAGE 01

    Mandate Alignment

    Defining role requirements, technical competencies, and team goals.

    STAGE 02

    Market Mapping

    Mapping target FinTech companies and identifying relevant professionals.

    STAGE 03

    Direct Outreach

    Discreet, confidential outreach leveraging trusted industry networks.

    STAGE 04

    Vetting & Assessment

    Multi-stage evaluation of technical track record and cultural fit.

    STAGE 05

    Offer & Onboarding

    Managing negotiations, counter-offers, and onboarding transition.
