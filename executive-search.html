    Engagement Options

    Retained & Contingent Search Models.

    We tailor our engagement structures based on mandate complexity, sensitivity, secrecy requirements, and timeline urgency.

    Retained Search

    Recommended for Board, CEO, CFO/CRO, and critical senior leadership mandates requiring exhaustive market coverage and absolute discretion.

    • Dedicated Senior Partner delivery & commitment
    • Exhaustive market mapping across competitor institutions
    • Strict Non-Disclosure & confidential direct outreach
    • Comprehensive candidate assessment & reference checks
    • Guaranteed search completion & placement support

    Contingent Search

    Well-suited for urgent operational roles, business head mandates, and specialized mid-to-senior talent pipelines where speed is critical.

    • Access to mapped candidate databases & referral networks
    • Rapid presentation of qualified talent profiles
    • Direct assessment against job competencies
    • Success-aligned fee model
    • Flexible deployment across mid and senior levels
    Our Process

    The 5-Stage Executive Search Methodology.

    STAGE 01

    Mandate Alignment

    We work closely with the Board, CEO, or CHRO to define the strategic mandate, key performance metrics, cognitive requirements, and cultural parameters of the target position.

    STAGE 02

    Market Mapping

    Our research team maps target institutions across India, the Middle East, and international markets, identifying potential candidates currently performing in equivalent or adjacent leadership roles.

    STAGE 03

    Confidential Outreach

    We initiate discreet, direct contact with prospective leaders, leveraging trusted industry networks and candidate relationships cultivated over years of financial services focus.

    STAGE 04

    Rigorous Assessment

    Candidates undergo structured multi-stage evaluations reviewing technical track record, risk awareness, leadership style, and alignment with client culture before presentation.

    STAGE 05

    Offer & Onboarding

    We advise on compensation structuring, assist in offer negotiations, manage counter-offer risks, and maintain engagement through the candidate's transition and onboarding phase.
