    Firm Profile & Leadership

    Intellect, discretion, and market precision.

    Founded in 2016 and based in Mumbai, NayaConnect Executive Search is a boutique advisory firm specializing exclusively in leadership hiring for Financial Services across India, the Middle East, and international markets.

    Firm Purpose

    Built exclusively for Financial Services.

    We believe executive search is a strategic partnership that directly shapes organizational performance, governance, and market reputation.

    Financial Services is an ecosystem governed by continuous regulatory evolution, risk management, and technology adoption. Finding leaders with both technical domain mastery and cultural adaptability requires a deliberate, research-driven approach.

    As a boutique firm, we do not delegate mandates to junior teams. Every search engagement receives senior partner oversight, ensuring absolute confidentiality, speed of execution, and uncompromised candidate quality.
