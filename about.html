    About NayaConnect • FinTech Recruitment

    Specialist recruitment for FinTech technology teams.

    NayaConnect is a specialist recruitment consultancy focused exclusively on the FinTech sector. We help FinTech companies find and hire the technology talent they need to build, innovate, and scale.

    Our Philosophy

    Understanding business, tech, and people.

    Successful hiring starts with understanding the business, the technology, and the team behind it. We focus on matching professionals with the exact technical skills and culture fit your company requires.

    We work with startups, scaleups, and established players across Payments, Neobanking, LendingTech, WealthTech, and InsurTech.
