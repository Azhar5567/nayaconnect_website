    Deep vertical expertise across all FinTech roles.

    We partner exclusively with organizations within the financial technology ecosystem, recruiting top talent across all seniority levels — from software engineers, product analysts, and risk specialists to team leads, functional directors, and C-Suite executives.

    FinTech Verticals & All Roles

    Specialized recruitment across all levels and disciplines.

    From entry and mid-level specialists to engineering leads, functional managers, and executive leadership.

    01. Payments & Commerce

    Gateways, POS & Remittances

    Recruiting talent across the entire organization — from payment engineers, API architects, and merchant onboarding specialists to product managers, compliance officers, and executive leadership (VPs, CTOs) — for high-throughput payment gateways, digital wallets, remittance networks, and POS terminals.

    Fullstack & API Engineers Product Managers & Analysts Merchant Ops & BD Associates Compliance & Fraud Analysts VP Engineering & Payment Heads

    02. Neobanking & Core Tech

    Digital Banks & Embedded Finance

    Placing talent across all functional levels — including mobile application developers, core banking software engineers, UI/UX designers, KYC/operations specialists, product owners, and C-suite leadership — for digital banks, neobanks, and embedded finance providers building next-generation banking rails.

    iOS, Android & Cloud Developers Core Banking Platform Engineers UI/UX & Product Designers KYC & Customer Ops Specialists Heads of Product & Tech (CPO/CTO)

    03. LendingTech & Credit

    Consumer, MSME & BNPL

    Sourcing professionals across every operational and leadership tier — including credit risk underwriters, data scientists, collections associates, loan product managers, and Chief Risk Officers — for digital lending apps, MSME credit platforms, Buy-Now-Pay-Later (BNPL) firms, and automated underwriting systems.

    Credit Risk & Underwriting Analysts Data Scientists & Risk Modelers Collections & Operations Associates Lending Product Managers Heads of Credit & CROs

    04. WealthTech

    Robo-Advisory & Broking Platforms

    Recruiting across all technical, quantitative, and management roles — from quantitative analysts, backend developers, and wealth relationship managers to product leads, Chief Technology Officers, and Chief Investment Officers — for algorithmic trading platforms, discount brokerages, and digital wealth managers.

    Quantitative Analysts & Algo Devs Full Stack & Frontend Engineers Wealth & Investment Associates Growth & Product Managers Chief Investment & Tech Officers

    05. InsurTech

    Digital Brokers & Claims Tech

    Placing talent across all career stages — including actuarial analysts, claims processing associates, insurance product managers, software developers, and executive leadership — for digital insurance distribution platforms, automated claims assessment SaaS, and embedded insurance scaleups.

    Actuarial Analysts & Associates Claims & Operations Executives Insurance Product Managers Strategic Partnership Managers Appointed Actuaries & Tech Leads

    06. RegTech & Compliance Tech

    KYC, AML & Fraud Detection SaaS

    Staffing all technical, analytical, operational, and commercial functions — from KYC/AML verification analysts, fraud investigators, and machine learning engineers to enterprise sales directors, compliance heads, and General Counsel — for financial crime prevention and regulatory software providers.

    AML & KYC Research Analysts Fraud Detection Specialists Machine Learning & Data Engineers Enterprise Account Executives Heads of Compliance & Legal Counsel
