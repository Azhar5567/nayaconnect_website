    Specialized search across core financial technology disciplines.

    Gateways, POS & Remittances

    Identifying payment gateway, digital wallet, cross-border remittance, POS tech, and merchant acquiring leadership capable of scaling high-throughput transaction processing while maintaining PCI-DSS and regulatory compliance.

    Digital Banks & Embedded Finance

    Placing senior executives for digital banks, neobanks, core banking software platforms, and embedded finance providers building next-generation digital customer accounts and API banking rails.

    Consumer, MSME & BNPL

    Securing modern credit leaders and risk engineers for consumer lending apps, MSME credit platforms, Buy-Now-Pay-Later (BNPL) platforms, and digital underwriting systems balancing loan book growth with low NPA ratios.

    Heads of Credit Risk Underwriting VP Engineering Chief Operating Officers Chief Financial Officers

    Robo-Advisory & Broking Platforms

    Appointing leaders for algorithmic trading platforms, discount broking apps, automated personal finance tools, and digital wealth management platforms managing retail and accredited investor portfolios.

    Chief Investment Officers Head of Product Growth Chief Information Security Officers Heads of Quantitative Research
